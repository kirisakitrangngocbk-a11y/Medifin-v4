How to play

What should you do?

Review the files, ask questions, diagnose the case, choose a plan, prepare its implementation, and reassess after new developments.

How to play

Select or clear cards before confirming. Each group shows how many choices are required. Once confirmed, choices are saved and the case advances.

Opened files and evidence appear in the notebook. Select an evidence title to read it, then select "Use as evidence" to cite it. Progress is saved automatically. "Restart" begins a new playthrough.

How is the score calculated?

The game evaluates the consulting process and the outcome. Process dimensions are investigation, reasoning and diagnosis, treatment suitability, communication, professional ethics, and adaptation. The total case score combines 50% process, 30% financial outcome, and 20% client experience. Governance is reported separately. Serious ethical violations may cap the process and total case score.

Decision guidance

Consider evidence sources, uncertainty, implementation conditions, and new developments. Reading evidence does not automatically cite it in your diagnosis.
